Alwafaa Lebanese School is pleased to announce that registration is now open
for the 2026–2027 academic year, across all academic stages.

## Available places

Applications are accepted subject to the places available in each grade. We
encourage families to apply early, particularly for kindergarten, as those
classes fill quickly every year.

## How to register

You are welcome to visit the school during office hours, or to call the
registration office to ask about requirements and the documents needed.

We look forward to welcoming your children into the Alwafaa family.
